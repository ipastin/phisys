# MyPhisys Brand Foundation

## 1. Canonical identity

- **Name:** MyPhisys
- **Pronunciation:** *my PHEE-sis* / «май фи́сис»
- **Semantic expansion:** **My Personal Health Intelligence System**
- **Category:** **Personal Health Intelligence**
- **Brand model:** one brand, one product
- **Primary domain target:** `myphisys.com` — target only, pending independent registrar/trademark clearance

The previous split-brand option `Phisys` (platform/masterbrand) + `MyPhisys` (consumer app) is **superseded**. All product, repository, documentation and user-facing naming should converge on **MyPhisys**.

## 2. Positioning

### Primary positioning statement

> **MyPhisys is a Personal Health Intelligence System that turns fragmented health records, measurements and everyday health data into a trustworthy longitudinal picture of your health — helping you understand what changed, what happened around it, and what may deserve your attention.**

### Short descriptor

> **Personal Health Intelligence**

### Primary tagline

> **Your health, understood.**

### Product line

> **Understand your health over time.**

### Core mechanism line

> **Know what changed. Understand why.**

## 3. Brand promise

> **MyPhisys helps you understand your health as a changing system, not a collection of isolated numbers.**

MyPhisys does not position itself as an AI doctor, autonomous clinician, diagnosis engine or treatment authority. AI is a component of the system, not the brand promise.

## 4. Brand pillars

### 4.1 Longitudinal
Health makes more sense over time: baseline, change, trend and history are more useful than isolated measurements.

### 4.2 Contextual
A number matters in context: personal baseline, nearby events, source quality and evidence remain visible.

### 4.3 Trustworthy
Facts, calculations, source records and AI interpretations remain semantically distinct and traceable.

### 4.4 Calm
Surface signal, not alarm. Avoid anxiety amplification, warning inflation and pseudo-clinical certainty.

## 5. Brand attributes

**Precise · Calm · Intelligent · Transparent · Personal**

Avoid positioning MyPhisys as playful, magical, futuristic-for-its-own-sake, biohacking-oriented or motivational-gamified.

## 6. Verbal identity

### Preferred vocabulary

### Avoid by default

- universal health/readiness score
- certainty where data is incomplete

## 7. Tone of voice

MyPhisys communicates with high information density but low emotional intensity.

Principles:

1. **Fact before interpretation.**
2. **Directional language before judgement.** `Increased` is not automatically `worse`.
3. **Explicit uncertainty.** Use `limited data`, `records don't agree`, `possible explanation`, `based on available records`.
4. **Actionable but non-authoritarian.** Help review, prepare, record and understand; do not impersonate clinical authority.
5. **No disclaimer spam.** Safety language is proportional to risk.

Example:

**Avoid:** `AI detected that poor sleep is causing your elevated heart rate.`

**Prefer:** `Your sleep duration decreased during the same period. The available data does not establish that one change caused the other.`

## 8. Product naming system

### Product

**MyPhisys**

### Core navigation

- Today
- Timeline
- Health
- Records
- Ask

### Core feature

**What Changed**

### Conversational interaction

**Ask MyPhisys**

Do not introduce a separate named assistant persona in v0.x. MyPhisys itself is the intelligence surface.

### Feature naming principles

Prefer descriptive names over branded sub-products. Avoid `MyPhisys Insights`, `MyPhisys Timeline`, etc. inside the single application unless a feature later becomes a separate product surface.

## 9. Visual territory

Primary visual direction: **Systemic Intelligence**.

Visual concepts:

- signals;
- relationships;
- time;
- longitudinal change;
- systems;
- evidence;
- calm precision.

Prefer a wordmark-first identity. If a symbol is introduced, explore abstract system/axis/longitudinal forms rather than literal healthcare iconography.

Avoid generic symbols:

- heart;
- ECG line;
- medical cross;
- DNA helix;
- brain;
- sparkle;
- AI-node network;
- shield;
- generic human silhouette.

## 10. Brand architecture constraints

1. `MyPhisys` is the canonical name in product UI, docs and repository naming.
2. `Phisys` is not a separate platform/masterbrand in v0.x.
3. `Health AI` may be used as a generic technical/category phrase only when needed, not as a product identifier.
4. The acronym/semantic expansion must not be styled as `M.P.H.I.S.` or otherwise forced into the wordmark.
5. Domain choice must not redefine the product name; `myphisys.com` is the preferred target, not the source of truth.
6. Legal/trademark/domain clearance is a separate release gate and is not implied by this specification.

## 11. UX implications

- Home remains insight-first: **Today → What Changed → Explore → Ask MyPhisys**.
- Do not make AI the homepage or primary value proposition.
- Provenance, context inspection and uncertainty are part of the brand experience, not purely technical features.
- Remove AI-slop patterns: gradients-as-meaning, sparkle branding, opaque health scores, generic `AI insight` cards and pseudo-scientific certainty.

## 12. Brand acceptance criteria

A user-facing surface is consistent with the MyPhisys brand when:

- the product name is MyPhisys;
- the copy leads with health understanding, not AI technology;
- claims preserve distinction between facts, calculations and interpretations;
- uncertainty is explicit where relevant;
- visual language remains calm and system-oriented;
- source/provenance remains reachable for consequential claims;
- no separate AI persona or split `Phisys` masterbrand is introduced.
