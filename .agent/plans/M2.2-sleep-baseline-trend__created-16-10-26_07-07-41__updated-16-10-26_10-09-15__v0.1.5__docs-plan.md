## 4. Scope / Non-goals

One synthetic device-derived daily metric in canonical hours. A fixed seven-local-date current period compares with the preceding 28 local dates, with no overlap. Reference median, raw MAD and inclusive Tukey-hinges IQR: for an odd reference sample, its median belongs to both lower and upper halves; each hinge is the median of its half. For the ordered sample 1–21, Q1=6, Q3=16 and IQR=10. Current median, absolute/relative delta and direction are the sign of the median comparison. Output carries quality, windows, versions, immutable parameters and provenance through nested Quality results. The integrated nonclinical Quality policy accepts explicit daily values 0–25 h and excludes impossible values. No clinical reference/target, health risk score, interpolation, slope, persistence/meaningfulness, other metric, UI, real records, LLM or deployment.

## 5. Invariants / Architecture impact

Read only current person-scoped M1 facts through the PR #7 read-only query. Quality gates run before a numerical result. Fewer than 21 observed reference dates, an incomplete current seven dates, invalid/excluded inputs within the combined 35-day window, overlapping day, or unproven source continuity returns `insufficient` with null baseline/trend. Valid dated facts outside that window have no effect, including on combined Quality provenance or exclusions. Undated or non-YYYY-MM-DD inputs remain visible to combined Quality as exclusions. Top-level `reasons` contains blocking codes and period-prefixed Quality causes such as `reference_incomplete_coverage`, `reference_stale` and `reference_unverified_inputs`; `limited` still permits a descriptive result. No canonical model, accepted ADR, clinical or privacy policy changes.

## 10. Decision log

16-10-26: selected the spec's 28 preceding days / seven current days, with at least 21 reference dates and a complete current week. A 14-day reference reacts faster but is more sensitive to noise; a 56-day reference is more stable but increases missingness and adaptation lag. These are versioned engineering parameters, not clinical thresholds. Selected strict suppression on any connector transition; separate source-specific baselines would be a larger slice, and combining sources without method-equivalence evidence would misstate comparability. The returned direction compares two medians only; it does not claim slope, persistence, benefit or harm.

16-10-26 review fix: chose inclusive Tukey hinges for odd reference samples, making the shared median part of both halves. Exclusive halves would give IQR=11 for 1–21; interpolated quantiles would introduce another convention. Inclusive hinges give IQR=10 and are locked by a golden test. Chose period-prefixed Quality reasons in the existing result field over a new output type, so clients can identify why a calculable result remains `limited` without changing the read-only API shape.

16-10-26 integration: selected a merge commit so published PR #8 history and SHA references remain intact. A rebase would rewrite the public branch; cherry-picking PR #7 fixes would duplicate commits and lose their direct provenance. The combined `sleep_duration` definition stays at 1.1.0 with frozen Quality and baseline parameters. Combined Quality now receives the full fact input because its pure function enforces the 35-day window; a second prefilter would duplicate window logic and could hide malformed dates.

16-10-26 per-fact integration: merged PR #7 head `51207aed1929fd75da613770a621707a1d8b5433` without rewriting PR #8 history. Resolved only two version conflicts to retain 1.1.0. Kept additive `provenance.factSupports`, existing aggregate provenance, the read-only DB reader, frozen Quality policy and 28/7/21 baseline parameters.

16-10-26 result isolation: merged PR #7 head `d4413e35e2525ecf072ecbb543b0461ebe5aaf52` without conflicts. Copying the top-level baseline result window closes the same mutable alias outside nested Quality results. Serialized values, version 1.1.0, frozen policies and 28/7/21 remain unchanged.
